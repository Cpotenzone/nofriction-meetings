noFriction Pro subscription

  • What it unlocks: the AI features: notes in the style of each recording type, follow-up email drafts and review guides, and on the Mac also chat across your recordings, live insights, reports, meeting prep briefs and screenshot analysis. Recording, transcription, recording types, Notebooks, timed recording, marks, links, calendar matching, people, photos and screenshots, search and export stay free.
  • Plans and prices: $0.99 a month or $5.99 a year, auto-renewing. noFriction Pro is offered in the United States. Prices are shown in the app and on the App Store before you buy.
  • Free trial: eligible new subscribers get a 1-week free trial. Its length is shown before you confirm. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription.
  • Payment: charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase (or when the free trial ends).
  • Renewal: the subscription renews automatically at the same price and period unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Managing and canceling: in your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions, in the App Store app, or from noFriction's Settings → Subscription. Canceling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
  • One purchase, all your devices: noFriction Pro works on iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. Use Restore Purchases in Settings → Subscription on a new device.
  • Refunds: Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your AI

noFriction offers no hosted AI models. AI features run on Apple's on-device model or on one OpenAI-compatible endpoint you enter yourself, with an optional key you supply. A noFriction Pro subscription doesn't include usage from any AI service: if your endpoint charges for use, its operator bills you directly, and its terms and privacy policy apply to what you send it. AI output can be wrong. Check notes, action items, emails and review guides before you rely on them or send them.

Recording laws

Many places require everyone in a conversation to agree before it is recorded. You are responsible for telling participants and for getting any consent the law requires before you record. Many schools require the instructor's permission to record a class; check your school's policy.

Your content

Your recordings, transcripts, photos, screenshots and notes are yours. They are stored on your device. We have no servers and no copy of them, so we can't recover them for you if you delete them or lose your device.
