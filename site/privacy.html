Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · applies to noFriction for iPhone, iPad and Mac

We don't collect your data. noFriction has no accounts and no servers of ours. It sends nothing to us.

What stays on your device

  • Audio recordings and transcripts. Transcription runs on your device.
  • Meeting titles, notes, photos, screenshots and the people list.
  • Calendar details the app reads to name meetings and list attendees. noFriction only reads your calendar; it never changes it.
  • Your settings and your AI provider API keys. Keys are kept in the system Keychain.

All of this stays in the app's storage on your device. Deleting a meeting deletes its data. Deleting the app deletes everything.

What leaves your device, and only when you ask

AI features are optional: notes, summaries, action items, follow-up emails, chat and briefings. When you use them, noFriction sends the text they need straight from your device to the AI provider you chose. Examples are OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, Groq, or a model on your own computer. The request uses your own API key. That text can include the transcript, the meeting title, attendee names, and screenshots for screen features. The app asks for your permission before it first sends anything to each provider.

That provider's own privacy policy and terms govern what it does with the data. noFriction never receives, stores or relays it. If you choose Apple's on-device model or a local model, nothing leaves your device or network.

On the Mac, you can also turn on optional cloud transcription with your own key (for example Deepgram). Audio is then sent to that provider. Local transcription is the default.

Purchases

Apple handles subscriptions. We see only what Apple provides to every developer: anonymous sales reports. We never receive your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No accounts, and no email address required.

Recording other people

Laws on recording conversations vary. In many places everyone in the conversation must agree to be recorded. You are responsible for telling participants and getting any consent the law requires.

Children

noFriction is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
