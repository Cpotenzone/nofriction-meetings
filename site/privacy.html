Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 · applies to noFriction for iPhone, iPad and Mac

noFriction has no app accounts and operates no hosted AI models or meeting-data servers. Meeting content is stored on your device. Optional custom-endpoint AI sends content directly to the destination you configure; the details below describe those transfers.

What is stored on your device

  • Audio recordings and transcripts. Transcription runs on your device: Apple's on-device speech recognition on iPhone and iPad, and a Whisper speech model on the Mac.
  • Meeting titles, notes, photos, screenshots and the people list. On the Mac, screenshots are taken only of the displays or windows you choose, while you record.
  • Calendar details the app reads to name meetings and list attendees. noFriction only reads your calendar; it never changes it.
  • Your settings and your AI provider API keys. Keys are kept in the system Keychain and are never shown again in full.
  • The Mac app's diagnostic log, which never contains transcript text or keys. It stays on your Mac unless you choose to send it to us.

Deciding when a meeting has ended (to stop a recording automatically) also happens entirely on your device, from the calendar, the microphone and the transcript.

Deleting and striking content

You can delete a whole meeting, or select words, lines, photos or screenshots and choose Delete or Strike from the record. Either one removes the content from the transcript, search, the saved audio recording on iPhone and iPad (replaced with silence), photos and screenshots, and future exports. AI notes made from it are flagged for regeneration, and distinctive phrases are also removed from them. On the Mac it is also purged from the app's own database backups. Strike can't be undone and leaves only a marker with the time, the date and an optional reason, never the content. Copies you already exported, shared or sent to an AI provider, photos still in your Photos library, and device backups (such as iCloud or Time Machine) are outside the app's control.

Use the app's deletion controls to remove local meetings before uninstalling. Removing a Mac app does not necessarily remove its application data, Keychain entries, exports or backups. Content already sent to a provider remains subject to that provider's retention and deletion policies.

What leaves your device

AI features, with the provider you choose

AI features are optional. On iPhone and iPad: meeting notes (summary, decisions, action items) and follow-up emails. On the Mac: chat across your meetings, live meeting insights, meeting reports, meeting prep briefs and screenshot analysis. noFriction offers no hosted AI models. Local and Apple on-device models support offline AI after setup and downloads. No remote service or credential is preconfigured. If you enter an OpenAI-compatible endpoint and model, the app sends the content it needs straight from your device to that endpoint, using an optional API key you supply. That text can include the transcript, the meeting title, attendee names, email addresses, companies and roster details, calendar invite notes and, for screen features on the Mac, screenshots. The app asks for your permission before it first sends meeting content to a public endpoint, and you can revoke that permission in Settings. Changing the endpoint clears its previous consent and stored key; a new destination must be reviewed again.

On the Mac, once you've connected and approved a provider, noFriction uses it automatically for live insights while you record and for a report after a recording longer than six minutes. You can turn either one off in Settings → AI Engine → Automatic AI. If Apple's on-device model is available and no provider is selected, the Mac can use Apple's model instead; that inference runs on your Mac. Unsupported previously saved provider presets remain unavailable and do not select another remote service.

That provider's own privacy policy and terms govern what it does with the data. noFriction never receives, stores or relays it. Apple on-device inference runs on this device. An endpoint on another computer receives content across your network, even when it is classified as local; its operator controls any further processing and retention.

On-device transcription

Transcription uses Apple's on-device speech recognition on iPhone and iPad, and Local Whisper on Mac. No cloud-transcription service is built in. Local model downloads may require internet access before offline use.

Downloads and links

The Mac app downloads its speech-recognition model once from Hugging Face. No meeting data is sent. Links you choose to open, such as a LinkedIn search for an attendee or the support page, open in your browser and are governed by those sites' policies.

Purchases

Apple handles subscriptions. We see only what Apple provides to every developer: sales and subscription reports that don't identify you. We never receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription with Apple on your device.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • noFriction does not sell your personal data or share it for advertising. Optional provider transfers, exports and reports you choose to share are described above.
  • No accounts, and no email address required.

If you choose to share crash reports with developers in your device settings, Apple gives us those reports without your identity.

Recording other people

Laws on recording conversations vary. In many places everyone in the conversation must agree to be recorded. You are responsible for telling participants and getting any consent the law requires.

Children

noFriction is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
