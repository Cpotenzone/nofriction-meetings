Guides · Classes

Capture a class and study from it

Stay in the lecture instead of copying it down. noFriction records it, keeps the slides next to what was said, and turns it into study material. The example: BIO 101, a 50-minute lecture on cellular respiration.

  • During class

  • Study
  • More ways to use it

Check your school's policy. Many schools require the instructor's permission to record a class, and some require classmates' consent. noFriction reminds you the first time you record a class.

Before class

Step 1

Choose Class and a notebook

Tap Record, choose Class, pick the length of the lecture (it stops by itself, with a warning and +15 min before it does) and type the course as the Notebook, for example "BIO 101". Next time, tap the notebook chip instead of typing.

A Class gets lecture notes and a study guide instead of meeting notes.

During class

Step 2

Mark what's on the test

When the instructor says "this will be on the exam", tap Mark this moment and choose On the test. Use Question for anything you want to ask later.

On an Apple Watch, Discreet dims the screen, and a tap anywhere marks the moment.

After class

Step 3

Read the lecture notes

Open the recording and tap Make notes. You get the key concepts, definitions with examples, and any announcements or deadlines the instructor gave.

Step 4

Jump to your marks

Marks lists every moment you marked. Filter to On the test and tap one to read exactly what was said.

Study

Step 5

Make a study guide

Tap Make study guide for a summary, key terms, flashcards, a practice quiz and questions to ask, with extra weight on what you marked On the test.

Flip each flashcard and tap Again or Known. Export them as a CSV to use in other study apps.

Step 6

Quiz yourself

The practice quiz explains every answer and can take you straight to the moment in the lecture where it was covered.

Step 7

Keep the whole course together

Every lecture recorded into "BIO 101" stays in that notebook. Tap the chip to see the course, or ask Chat about this notebook: "What did the professor say about the midterm?"

More ways to use it

Lab safety briefing

Record it as a Class in the course notebook and mark every rule as Important. The notes list them in order.

Office hours

Mark each answer you need as Question, then search a word from it before the exam.

Online lectures

On a Mac, choose the browser window with the slides as the screen to capture. Rewind shows the slide that was up when each line was said.
