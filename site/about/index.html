The app

noFriction records meetings, classes and everyday life, transcribes on your device and lets you rewind to any moment. It was built on one rule: nothing leaves your device unless you want it to. There are no noFriction servers, no accounts and no analytics; AI is Apple's on-device model or one endpoint you enter yourself. See what it does, or get notified at launch.
