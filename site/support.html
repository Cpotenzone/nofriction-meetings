Support

noFriction records and transcribes your meetings on your device. Optional AI writes notes using Apple on-device or an endpoint and model you configure.

Contact

Email casey@nofriction.io. We usually reply within two business days. Tell us your device, its OS version and the app version (iPhone/iPad: Settings tab → About; Mac: noFriction Meetings → About). Please don't send recordings or transcripts unless we ask; we never need your API key.

Using AI offline

noFriction offers no hosted AI models. Use Apple's on-device model on a compatible device with Apple Intelligence enabled (iOS 26 or macOS 26 and later), or enter the URL and model of your own local OpenAI-compatible endpoint in AI settings. Finish required model downloads before going offline. A model on another computer needs a reachable local network. AI features require noFriction Pro.

Connecting your own AI endpoint

  1. Choose an OpenAI-compatible server yourself and obtain its base URL, model name and any required API key. The app includes no default service or key.
  2. On iPhone and iPad open the Settings tab. On Mac open Settings → AI Engine. Enter the endpoint, model and optional key, then save. Saving sends no test request and does not infer a service from the key.
  3. Before meeting content is sent to a public endpoint, review the destination and consent. You can revoke consent in Settings. Changing the endpoint clears its old key and approval.

Keys are stored in the system Keychain. Your selected endpoint operator sets any usage charges and retention policy. Public endpoints require HTTPS; approved local addresses may use HTTP. Apple on-device needs no key. Previously saved named service presets must be configured again as an explicit custom endpoint.

Common questions

"Wrong key" or "no credit"

Check that you copied the whole key and that your provider account has billing set up.

Transcription isn't working

Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition in Settings → Privacy & Security. On a Mac, also allow Screen & System Audio Recording so other participants are captured. On a Mac, the first recording downloads the on-device speech model once; keep the Mac online for that.

The recording stopped by itself

noFriction can stop a recording when the meeting ends: when the calendar event is over and the room goes quiet, or after a few minutes with nobody speaking. It shows a 30-second banner first, so you can keep recording. Everything said before the stop is saved. Turn it off or change the silence time in Settings → Recording (iPhone/iPad: Settings tab; Mac: Settings → General).

Removing something from a meeting

Select words, lines or a screenshot/photo and choose Delete (no trace, 5 seconds to undo) or Strike from the record (permanent, leaves a marker with the time and an optional reason). Both remove the content from the transcript, search, the saved audio (iPhone and iPad) and future exports, and flag AI notes made before the edit. Copies you already shared or sent to an AI provider can't be recalled.

Restoring a subscription

Open Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with. One subscription covers iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Canceling

Manage or cancel any time from Settings → Subscription in the app, or in your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Moving to a new device

Meetings are stored only on the device that recorded them; noFriction has no cloud sync. To keep a copy elsewhere: on iPhone and iPad, use the Share button in a meeting; on the Mac, export to an Obsidian vault (Settings → Obsidian) or export your data (Settings → Data).
