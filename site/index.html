Contact

Email support@nofriction.ai. We usually reply within two business days.

Connecting an AI provider

  1. Get an API key from your provider. For example: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, xAI Grok or Groq.
  2. In noFriction, open Settings → AI and paste the key. The app recognizes the provider and checks that the key works.
  3. Choose a model. You're done.

Your provider bills you directly for what you use. noFriction never sees your key or your data. To use a model on your own computer, choose Ollama, LM Studio or Custom and enter its address.

Common questions

"Wrong key" or "no credit"

Check that you copied the whole key and that your provider account has billing set up.

Transcription isn't working

Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition in Settings → Privacy & Security. On a Mac, also allow Screen & System Audio Recording to capture other participants.

Restoring a subscription

Open Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Canceling

Manage or cancel any time in Settings → Subscription → Manage, or in your Apple Account settings.
