How it works

Five steps, no sign-up

Everything up to the notes runs on your device. AI is Apple's on-device model, or one endpoint you enter yourself.

  1. 1

    Record

    Tap Record on your iPhone, Apple Watch or Mac. Say what it is: Meeting, Class or Personal, and how long.

  2. 2

    Transcribed on your device

    The transcript appears as people talk. Apple speech recognition on iPhone and iPad, a local speech model on the Mac. No cloud.

  3. 3

    Mark what matters

    One tap marks a moment Important. Pick Question, or Follow up, On the test or Remember, and add a note.

  4. 4

    Rewind

    Go back to any moment. On the Mac, Rewind shows the screen that was up next to what was said.

  5. 5

    Notes and review

    Notes in the style of the recording, and a review guide with flashcards and a practice quiz. With Apple on-device AI or your own endpoint.

Meetings, classes and everyday life

One app for all of it

Say what the recording is and noFriction follows: the notes, the third mark and the review guide change with the type.

Meetings

Record the call or the room. Calendar matching names the meeting and lists who was there. Mark Follow up as you go.

You leave with decisions, action items and a follow-up email draft, with every claim traceable to the moment it was said.

Classes

Set a 60- or 90-minute limit, put it in a Notebook like "BIO 101", and mark On the test when the instructor says so.

Before the exam: a study guide with key terms, flashcards and a practice quiz that jumps to the moment the professor explained it.

Everyday life

A doctor's visit, a contractor walkthrough, a talk, an idea on the drive home. Personal covers everything else. Mark Remember on the detail you'd lose.

A summary, the key points and the to-dos, and the exact words when you need them: "order the tiles by Friday".

Rewind

Go back to any moment

On the Mac, noFriction keeps the screens you choose next to the transcript. Pick any second of a meeting or a lecture and see the slide that was up and the words that went with it.

  • Marks sit on the timeline and in the transcript. Filter the list to On the test before an exam, or Follow up after a meeting.
  • Search every transcript. Play the audio from any line.
  • On iPhone and iPad, add photos of slides and whiteboards; they land in the timeline next to what was said.

Mark this moment

One tap for Important. Then Question, or the mark for the type: Follow up for a meeting, On the test for a class, Remember for everything else, with a note. On the Mac, Control-Option-Command-M marks even while another app is in front.

Notebooks

Group recordings into "Acme project", "BIO 101" or "Health". Filter chips show one Notebook at a time. On Apple Watch, pick from your recent Notebooks before you record.

Timed recording

Choose 15, 30, 60 or 90 minutes, or no limit. It stops by itself at the end, after a warning with +15 min. Meetings can also stop when the calendar event ends and the room goes quiet.

Links

Web addresses said in a recording appear in its Links list with the time they were said. Add your own references: the agenda, the reading, the deck. Nothing is fetched.

Review guides (Pro)

Summary, key terms, flashcards, a practice quiz with explanations and Jump to this moment, and questions to ask. Called a study guide for a class. Export flashcards as a CSV file that popular flashcard apps can import, or the guide as Markdown.

Edit, delete, or strike from the record

Fix a word. Delete a line with five seconds to undo. Or Strike from the record: the words are removed everywhere, including the saved audio, and only a marker remains. Share any recording as text, export flashcards and guides, or export every recording as Markdown on the Mac.

On iPhone and iPad

Apple Watch

Record from your wrist. Transcribed on your iPhone.

The watch only records. Say what it is, how long and which Notebook, mark moments with a tap, and the audio goes to your iPhone, which transcribes it on the device. The watch keeps each recording until your iPhone confirms it arrived.

Privacy

No servers. No account. Data Not Collected.

On your device

  • Audio, transcripts, photos, screenshots, marks and notes are stored on your device.
  • Transcription runs on the device: Apple speech recognition on iPhone and iPad, a local speech model on the Mac. Watch recordings are transcribed on your iPhone.
  • No noFriction servers, no analytics, no tracking, no account. Apple's privacy label: Data Not Collected.

AI is yours to choose

noFriction hosts no AI. Use Apple's on-device model (iOS 26 or macOS 26 and later, with Apple Intelligence), and nothing leaves the device. Or enter one OpenAI-compatible endpoint, with your own key if it needs one: a model on your Mac, on your network, or a service you have an account with. The app asks before it sends anything to a public endpoint and shows the destination. Keys live in the Keychain and are never shown again.

FAQ

Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Recording and transcription run on your device with no connection. On the Mac, the speech model is downloaded once during setup. AI also works offline with Apple's on-device model, or with a model running on your own computer.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for and no noFriction server. Subscriptions go through your Apple Account.

What does "Data Not Collected" mean?

It is Apple's privacy-label term: the app sends nothing to us or to any partner. There are no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking. Content leaves your device only when you choose: to the AI endpoint you set up, in an export or share, or from your Apple Watch to your own iPhone. Privacy policy.

Which AI does it use?

Your choice of two: Apple's on-device model (iOS 26 or macOS 26 and later on a device with Apple Intelligence), or one OpenAI-compatible endpoint you enter yourself: its base URL, model name and, if it needs one, your key. noFriction hosts no AI model and includes no service. The app asks before sending recording content to a public endpoint.

What is free, and what needs Pro?

Free: recording, on-device transcription, types, Notebooks, timed recording, marks, links, Rewind, search, editing and export. Pro: the AI features, which are notes, follow-up email drafts and review guides. Pro is $0.99 a month or $5.99 a year with a 1-week free trial, in the United States.

How does the Apple Watch app work?

The watch records; your iPhone transcribes. Choose the type, length and Notebook on the watch, mark moments with a tap, and the audio is sent to your iPhone in the background. The watch deletes its copy only after the iPhone confirms it has it. Discreet mode keeps the watch screen nearly black for lectures and quiet rooms.

Can I use it in class?

Many schools require the instructor's permission to record a class, and some require classmates' consent. Check your school's policy. noFriction reminds you the first time you record a class.

Is recording people legal?

Laws vary. In many places everyone in the conversation must agree. noFriction reminds you to tell people; getting consent is up to you.

Can I get my recordings out?

Yes. Share any recording as text from iPhone and iPad. On the Mac, export recordings as Markdown to a folder you choose, or everything as JSON. Flashcards export as CSV and review guides as Markdown.

Do recordings sync between devices?

No. There is no cloud, so each device keeps what it recorded. Your Pro subscription works on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
