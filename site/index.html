Every meeting, written down. Nothing sent to us.

What it does

Built for people who live in meetings

Record and transcribe on your device

Live transcript as people talk. On iPhone and iPad it uses Apple's on-device speech recognition and keeps recording with the screen locked. On the Mac it runs a Whisper model locally and captures your microphone and the other participants' audio.

Knows the meeting and the people

Each recording is matched to your calendar event, so it gets a real name and an attendee list. Keep a people list across meetings and link each person's LinkedIn profile.

Keep the slides, not just the words

On the Mac, pick the displays or windows to capture; noFriction saves a screenshot when they change, and Snap saves one on demand. On iPhone and iPad, take photos of slides and whiteboards or add screenshots from Photos.

Notes and follow-ups, with your AI

On iPhone and iPad: one tap for a summary, decisions and action items that only name owners and dates actually said, plus a follow-up email draft. On the Mac: ask questions across all your meetings, get live insights while you talk, and an automatic report after each meeting.

Delete, or strike from the record

Remove words, lines or screens. Delete leaves no trace and gives you five seconds to undo. Strike from the record destroys the content everywhere it's stored, including saved audio, and leaves only a marker with the time and an optional reason.

Stops when the meeting does

When the calendar event is over and the room goes quiet, or nobody has spoken for a few minutes, noFriction offers to stop. A 30-second banner lets you keep going. On the Mac it also notices when the call app lets go of the microphone or the meeting window closes.

How it works

Three steps, no sign-up

1. Record

Press Record when the meeting starts. noFriction reminds you to tell people you're recording.

2. Read along

The transcript appears as people talk, with photos or screenshots in the timeline.

3. Get the notes

Connect your AI once. Then get a summary, action items and follow-ups, or ask your meetings a question on the Mac.

Privacy and your own key

We run no servers. Your meetings never pass through us.

On your device

  • Audio, transcripts, photos and screenshots are stored only on your device.
  • Transcription runs on the device.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.
  • API keys are kept in the system Keychain.

Bring your own AI

Paste a key and noFriction recognizes the provider and checks it. Before it first sends a meeting to a cloud provider, it asks you, and says what will be sent. The provider bills you directly, under its own terms. Prefer nothing leaves? Use a local model or Apple's on-device model.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Subscriptions go through your Apple Account.

Do I need an AI key?

Not to record and transcribe. For AI notes you need noFriction Pro plus one of: an API key from a provider such as OpenAI, a local model (Ollama, LM Studio), or Apple's on-device model on a device that supports Apple Intelligence.

What does the AI provider see?

The transcript and the meeting's title, attendee names and invite notes (and on the Mac, screenshots for screen features), when an AI feature runs. On the Mac, once you've connected a provider, live insights and the after-meeting report use it automatically. It goes straight from your device to that provider. noFriction never sees it.

Does it work offline?

Recording and transcription do. On the Mac, the speech model downloads once on first use. AI features need your provider, unless you use a local or on-device model.

Is recording people legal?

It depends on where you and they are. In many places everyone must agree. noFriction reminds you to tell people; getting consent is up to you.

Can I get my meetings out?

Yes. On iPhone and iPad, share any meeting as text from the meeting view. On the Mac, export meetings as Markdown into an Obsidian vault, or export your data as JSON.

Do my meetings sync between devices?

No. Each device keeps its own meetings. Your subscription works on all of them.
