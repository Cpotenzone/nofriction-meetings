On the Mac: chat across your meetings, AI live insights, meeting reports, meeting prep briefs and screenshot analysis. Your AI provider

AI features run on the AI service you choose with your own API key (for example OpenAI, Anthropic or Google), on a model you run yourself, or on Apple's on-device model. A noFriction Pro subscription doesn't include usage from a third-party AI provider: that provider bills you directly, and its own terms and privacy policy apply to what you send it. AI output can be wrong. Check notes, action items and emails before you rely on them or send them.

Recording laws

Many places require everyone in a conversation to agree before it is recorded. You are responsible for telling participants and for getting any consent the law requires before you record.
