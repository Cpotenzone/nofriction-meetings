Beta builds are free, including noFriction Pro. Feedback with a screenshot through TestFlight reaches us directly, or email casey@nofriction.io.

Requirements

  • iPhone and iPad: iOS or iPadOS 18 or later.
  • Apple Watch: watchOS 10 or later, paired with an iPhone running noFriction.
  • Mac: macOS 12.3 or later. The speech model (about 550 MB) downloads once during setup.
  • Apple on-device AI: iOS 26 or macOS 26 or later on a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on. Otherwise, enter your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
